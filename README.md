 # 🚀 Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

> An interactive, AI-powered space mission simulation platform designed for junior astronauts and space enthusiasts, utilizing open NASA datasets and computer vision. Developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer** is an intelligent simulation system aimed at training candidate astronauts through three core training pillars:
1. **Physical & EVA Simulation:** Extravehicular activity simulation, physical fitness tracking, and physiological response monitoring.
2. **Operational & Docking Skills:** Vehicle navigation, landing, and ISS docking simulations.
3. **AI Mission Director & Flight Control:** A RAG-powered intelligent chatbot trained on official NASA manuals for emergency guidance and real-time support.
